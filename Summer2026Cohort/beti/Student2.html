Habitat of Animals and Plants

Habitat | In water

The animals and plants all have a similar habitat which is the ocean but some have both land and the ocean.

  1. Jellyfish -Jelly live in the water only because they breathe by absorbing dissolved oxygen direactly through their thin outer skin layer in a process called diffusion.
  2. Whales- Whales live mostly in the water but they come above water so they can breathe air because they are mammals.
  3. Sharks Sharks live only in the water because they can already breathe through their gills and if they are on land they will suffocate and overheat.
  4. Seahorses Penguins live can only survive in the water but they do live near the calm, shallow areas of the ocean.

Habitat of Plants

The plants all have a similar habitat with animals which is the ocean but they live in either land or ocean.

  • Trees - they live on land but the still need water to survive because it is animportant part of doing phototsynthesis.
  • Seagrasses - they were land plants before they evolved into the ocean like algae.
