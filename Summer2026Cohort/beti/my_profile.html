Ocean

I will be talking about the amazing ocean and also the plants and animals that live in the ocean.

Animals

There are many animals that live in the ocean but some of them are sea turtles, jellyfish, sharks, and whales.

Sea Turtles

Sea turles are ocean repties that have long flippers, instead of legs to "fly" in the water.

Sharks

Sharks are ocean fish that have no bones at all in their bodies and they have incredible smell.

Plants


There are many plants that live in the ocean but some of them are seagrass, kelp, seaweed, and plankton.

Seagrasses

Seagrasses are ocean plants that live completley underwater and in the shallow part of the ocean.

Kelp

Kelp is underwater seaweed that can grow super fast to make or form large oceans in the ocean.
