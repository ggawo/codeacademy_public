The Sound of Formula 1

Speed and Sound Tracks

Music is huge in Formula 1 racing. Before the lights go out, drivers like Lewis Hamilton or Max Verstappen listen to heavy beats on their headphones to block out the noise of the crowd and get totally focused. F1 edits their YouTube highlights and TV intros with epic, fast-paced electronic music to make the cars feel even faster and more dangerous! Plus, during the podium celebrations, they play national anthems which is a huge musical tradition.
