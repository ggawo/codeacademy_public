Our Project Research

To build this website, we did research using Wikipedia to learn how music, high-speed racing, and team sports all connect! Below are the official encyclopedic summaries and history facts for our three main topics. Click the gold links to read the full articles on the official Wikipedia site.

🎵 Music Article Summary

According to Wikipedia, music is the arrangement of sound to create combinations of form, harmony, melody, and rhythm. It is a "cultural universal," meaning it is found in literally every single human society on Earth! The modern English word actually comes from the Ancient Greek phrase "mousiké technē", which translates to the "art of the Muses". The Muses were nine mythological Greek goddesses who inspired all arts and sciences.

🏎️ Formula One Article Summary

Wikipedia defines Formula One (also called F1) as the highest class of international racing for open-wheel, single-seater formula cars. The championship started all the way back in 1950! The word "formula" is used because there is a super strict set of technical rules that all mechanical engineers and participant cars must follow. F1 cars are the fastest regulated road-course racing cars in the world because their massive front and rear wings create aerodynamic downforce to push them into the ground around sharp corners.

🏐 Volleyball Article Summary

According to Wikipedia, volleyball is a worldwide team sport where two teams of six players are separated by a net. It was invented in 1895 by an American educator named William G. Morgan in Holyoke, Massachusetts. He originally named the game "mintonette" because he took inspiration from badminton. He wanted to create a sport for businessmen at the YMCA that was fun but way less physically demanding and aggressive than basketball!
