Why Music Matters in Volleyball and F1

Hyping Up the Volleyball Court

If you have ever been to a volleyball game, you know the gym is never quiet! Music always plays during warmups to get the team's arms moving and energy high. In professional beach volleyball, it is even crazier because a live DJ plays music during the actual match right up until the server tosses the ball. The fast beat of the songs completely matches the quick pace of the sets, spikes, and digs, keeping the crowd loud and the players super hyped up.

The Soundtrack of F1 Racing

Formula 1 racing is super loud, but music is still a giant part of it. Before the race even starts, they play Brian Tyler's official F1 theme song on TV, which gets everyone excited for lights out. Drivers also wear giant headphones on the track grid to listen to their own playlists. It helps them block out the noise and get completely focused so they can shift gears and make sharp turns at like 200 miles per hour!
