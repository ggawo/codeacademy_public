Information about the Creator of this Website

The creator of this website is Amron Thomas. Amron is an Ethiopian-American boy born in Danbury, Connecticut, 13 years ago. He loves coding and hopes to work in technology and information technology (IT) later in his career. At the age of 9, Amron wrote his first code using a website created by MIT called Scratch. He wants to build websites for school, fun, or business because it helps improve his problem-solving skills, prepares him for future careers, and helps him focus. Amron created this website to give people an idea about the African country of Ethiopia and to show how he could combine his heritage and hobbies. His first HTML and CSS coding class using visual Studio is with a teacher named Getenet, who was a professor at Penn State.
