Argentina have won 3 world cups

Argentina have won 3 world cups. The first time Argentina won the world cup was in 1978 when Argentina beat Netherlands at Argentina.

The second time Argentina won the world cup was in 1986 when Argentina beat West Germany 3-2, and Diego Maradona lifted the golden trophey for Argentina.

The latest World Cup final that Argentina won was the 2022 World cup in Qatar. The 2022 World cup final is widley considered the best and most exciting final in soccer history. Argentina played France and there was no decisive winner after regulation and extra time so the game went on to penalties. 4 penalties were tooken by both teams, and Argentina's goalkeeper Emiliano Martinez managed to save 2 penalties.
